02 about me

I’m a front‑end developer with a growing passion for UI and web design. I care about building digital experiences that feel clear, structured, and thoughtfully crafted.

I’m driven by precision and clarity — the kind of design that feels effortless because every detail has purpose. I value warmth and approachability, bringing personality into my projects through colour, typography, and layout.

As of now, I am currently expanding my skills into UI/UX, design systems, and brand identity.
